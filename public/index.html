Cyclic Quadrilaterals and Power of a Point

Geometry ค่าย 1

A quadrilateral is called cyclic if all four vertices lie on a single circle.

Theorem 1

In a cyclic quadrilateral $ABCD$:

$$\angle A + \angle C = \angle B + \angle D = 180^\circ$$

Power of a Point

The power of point $P$ with respect to a circle with center $O$ and radius $r$:

$$\text{Pow}(P) = PO^2 - r^2$$
